THE THERMAL LANDSCAPE

LCZ classes strongly structure daytime heat

Fourteen WUDAPT LCZ types are mapped across Izmir Province. LCZ class explains 37.3% of 2025 summer daytime LST variance (Kruskal-Wallis eta-squared = 0.373), and the ordering holds in all thirteen summers 2014–2026.

A global OLS benchmark explains 78.2% of LST variance, but the residual Moran's I of 0.724 shows strong spatial structure. MGWR reduces residual dependence to a weak negative value and predicts the grid cells outside its calibration sample with R squared 0.839; under spatial block cross-validation a full global OLS predicts better, so MGWR is read descriptively.
